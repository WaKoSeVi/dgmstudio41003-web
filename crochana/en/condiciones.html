Translation of the original in Spanish. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

Last updated: 27 October 2026.

1. What Crochana is

Crochana is an app that calculates crochet patterns from your gauge swatch and your measurements, and shows how the stitches are made. It is developed by DGMStudio41003 (hereinafter, “we”). By using it you accept these terms.

2. What you crochet is yours

The patterns Crochana calculates come from your measurements and your swatch, and they are yours. You may crochet them, give them away and sell what you make with them, without asking our permission, crediting us or paying us anything. The only exception is the one in section 4: if the pattern includes a picture made from someone else’s image, selling or sharing it may require their permission.

3. The calculations

Crochana calculates with published formulas and tables, which we cite in the app’s documentation, and with the swatch you give it. Everyone crochets differently, and the yarn, the hook and the tension change the result: measure your swatch and try the garment on as you make it. The patterns are provided as they are, as an aid, and we do not guarantee that a garment will fit exactly.

This includes copyright, trademarks (for example, crests and logos of clubs or companies) and the image rights of the people who appear in it.

  3. Crocheting a picture of a protected image for yourself and selling or sharing what you make are not the same: the latter may require a licence from whoever holds the rights. Crochana does not grant any licence over third-party images and does not check that you have one.

  4. The image does not leave your phone. Crochana reads it on the phone itself, turns it into a chart and discards it: it is not stored, it is not sent to any server and neither we nor anyone else can see it. The only thing stored, if you save the pattern, is the resulting colour chart, on your phone and in the backups you make.

The free version crochets the hat, with an ad before the pattern. Crochana Premium unlocks everything else and removes the ad. It is bought inside the app, through Google Play, which charges, applies taxes and handles refunds according to its terms and the law:
