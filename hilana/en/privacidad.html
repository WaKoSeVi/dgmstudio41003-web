Hilana privacy policy

Translation of the original in Spanish. If there is any difference, the Spanish version prevails.

Last updated: 29 October 2026.

Controller

DGMStudio41003 (Spain). Contact: dgmstudio41003@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your device

Measurements, swatches, patterns, notes and settings are stored on the device and are not sent to DGMStudio41003. There are no user accounts.

If you have the system backup turned on, Android or iOS include the app's data in it, in your Google or iCloud account, under their own terms. The files you export (PDFs, app backups) are stored wherever you choose.

Data that is processed

Processing Data Recipient Legal basis Retention
Crash reports and usage statistics (Android only, only if you agree) Device model, system and app version, screens used, type of garment generated, random installation identifier Google Ireland Limited (Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase), as processor Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) Statistics: 2 months from last use. On the iPhone no crash or usage data is processed, and the purchase is verified on the device itself without sending data to DGMStudio41003.

Payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store with your account; DGMStudio41003 receives no payment data.

Consent

Crash reports and statistics are requested when the app is opened and can be changed in Settings → Help improve the app. Personalised ads are requested at start-up, where the law requires it, and can be changed in Settings → Ad privacy options; on the iPhone, also in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Your rights

You can delete the data on the device in Settings → Delete all my data or by uninstalling the app. System backups are managed from your Google or iCloud account.

The data sent to Google carries no personal identifiers, so it is not possible to attribute it to a specific person; withdrawing consent deletes the installation identifier and stops any further sending.

To exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, write to dgmstudio41003@gmail.com. You can lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with their date.
